## Design Context

### Users
DragonMine Z players on Windows who want to run the Shadow Boxing memory bot
without using a terminal. They need immediate confidence that the game window
was found, the detector is working, and keyboard input is being sent at the
selected speed. The app is intended for both the project creator and public
GitHub users with varied technical experience.

### Brand Personality
Energetic, technical, and confident. The interface should feel like a focused
combat instrument or scouter rather than a generic settings dashboard. Its
voice is concise and operational: clear states, short labels, and useful live
feedback.

### Aesthetic Direction
A distinctive gamer HUD influenced by the energy and instrumentation of anime
combat interfaces, without copying official Dragon Ball logos, characters, or
assets. Use sharply composed geometry, directional motifs, warm yellow and
charged green accents, and tinted neutrals. Support equally intentional light
and dark themes. Avoid generic neon-purple gaming aesthetics, glassmorphism,
template card grids, and decorative sci-fi clutter.

### Design Principles
1. Make operational state unmistakable: ready, searching, memorizing, sending,
   paused, and error states must read at a glance without relying on color alone.
2. Keep the primary loop immediate: find game, choose speed, start, observe,
   stop. Advanced detector controls stay progressively disclosed.
3. Treat the live sequence as the visual signature of the app, using the four
   established direction colors consistently and accessibly.
4. Preserve performance and restraint: purposeful motion, compact assets, fast
   startup, reduced-motion support, and no ornamental effects that tax the GPU.
5. Design both themes independently enough to maintain contrast, hierarchy,
   and the same energetic personality in bright and dark environments.
